What we heard

The brief

A small, native-feeling Windows 11 utility that shuts the PC down at a clock time. It may grow into a suite of single-purpose utilities (like PowerToys), so the mark must also work as a system: every future tool is a key with its own glyph and hue.

nativequietprecisedependablelightweight

Success: reads as power + time without a label, holds at 16 px in the taskbar, passes 3:1 on light and dark themes, and extends to a family of tool icons.

Final identity

Keycap

Every utility is a key you press once; this key's glyph is a power symbol read as a clock at 3:00.

  • Literal metaphor, as Microsoft's app-icon guidance asks: power + time, no letters
  • Suite-ready: future tools share the key and white glyph, each with its own hue
  • Pixel-fitted 16 px cut for the taskbar and title bar; face passes 3:1 on both themes
  • Selawik Semibold wordmark (OFL, Microsoft's open Segoe UI companion), outlined
